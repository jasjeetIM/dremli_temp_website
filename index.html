Scope

One workspace for the front-office patient journey.

Dremli supports the operational work around the visit: scheduling, intake, reminders, patient messages, attachments, and staff review. It stays focused on patient access and engagement, not billing, insurance operations, payments, or clinical record ownership.

Customer

Dental practices that want a cleaner patient experience without making the front desk reconcile multiple separate tools.

Problem

Scheduling, forms, texts, emails, attachments, and patient history often live in different places, so every follow-up starts with rebuilding context.

Solution

Dremli connects online scheduling, configurable intake, patient activity, and SMS and email follow-up into a practical workspace.

Outcome

Patients get a smoother path to care, and staff know what is scheduled, what is missing, and who needs a response.

Workflow

How Dremli supports the patient journey.

The platform is built around the work dental teams repeat every day: help patients book, collect intake, track readiness, communicate clearly, and keep the patient record easy to act on.

  1. Online booking
  2. Appointment scheduling
  3. Digital intake
  4. Attachments and consent
  5. SMS and email
  6. Visit readiness
  7. Patient follow-up

Home, Patients, Appointments, and Forms all share the same patient context, so staff can move from an activity item to the appointment, form submission, message history, or patient record without rebuilding the story.

Why Dremli

Broad enough for engagement, focused enough for daily use.

Dremli is intentionally centered on the front-office patient experience. It gives teams useful scheduling, communication, and intake workflows without drifting into every system a practice already owns.

  • Home activity feed for bookings, forms, messages, and follow-up
  • Appointment workspace for readiness, missing items, and schedule context
  • Forms workspace for booking links, intake fields, policies, and reminders
  • Patient profiles with appointments, attachments, SMS, and email history
