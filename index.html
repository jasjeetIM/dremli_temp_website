Overview

Front-office work, connected around the patient.

Dremli stays focused on the work around a visit: scheduling, intake, communication, attachments, and staff review. The workspace keeps context close so teams can act without toggling between disconnected tools.

01

Book the visit

Patients choose a visit type, provider, and available time from a practice-branded public form.

02

Collect the details

Digital intake captures demographics, visit notes, attachments, consent, and configurable fields.

03

Follow up clearly

Staff can send SMS or email from the same patient context used for forms and appointments.

Workflow

Designed around the repeatable path from request to visit.

Dremli makes the journey easy to follow by exposing each operational step from the first request through visit readiness and patient follow-up.

Workspace

Home, Patients, Appointments, and Forms share the same context.

The product surfaces the operational state staff need: what was scheduled, which forms were filled, what needs review, and how to reach the patient.

Home activity feed for bookings, forms, messages, and follow-up Patient profiles with appointments, attachments, SMS, and email history Appointment workspace for readiness, missing items, and schedule context Forms workspace for booking links, intake fields, policies, and reminders

PMS-aware

Built to fit the systems dental teams already rely on.

Connector-aware

Designed for PMS-backed availability, appointment writeback, and patient context.

Communication-ready

SMS and email use a durable outbox so staff actions can be tracked and dispatched reliably.

Scoped by design

Focused on patient engagement workflows rather than replacing the clinical or billing system.
